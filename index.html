Why do the peaks move?

In affine sweep s, the local sampling time is p = asi + bsj + cs. The Doppler phase becomes a spatial-frequency shift. Two different slope pairs create two different peak positions for the same target. At zero Doppler, both spectra peak at the physical spatial frequency.

θx,s = ωx + asγ
θy,s = ωy + bsγ
γ = 2πνT
What is being plotted? Each heatmap is the exact noiseless single-target, normalized matched-filter PSF evaluated on a spatial-frequency grid for the chosen finite switching schedule: Ps(k) = |(1/Ms) Σp exp(j2π[(kx,s − kx)ip/Nx + (ky,s − ky)jp/Ny])|². The combined panel is a color overlay of the two individually calculated PSFs, not a jointly estimated spectrum. No target detection or full angle–Doppler estimator is used.
