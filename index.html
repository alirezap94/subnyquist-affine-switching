The central idea

Design the sampling trajectory. Simplify the estimation.

With a single RF chain, a planar antenna array is sampled sequentially. Carefully chosen affine switching schedules turn the Doppler-dependent temporal phase into a predictable spatial-frequency shift.

01 / MEASURE

Switch along affine patterns

Collect sub-Nyquist antenna–time measurements across two switching sweeps with different slopes.

02 / ESTIMATE

Find two spatial spectra

Estimate the shifted two-dimensional spatial frequencies separately for each sweep.

03 / RECOVER

Recover direction and Doppler

Associate the spectral peaks across sweeps, then use the known affine slopes to recover the physical parameters.
